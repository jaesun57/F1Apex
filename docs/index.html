What these results are not

  • Not validated. Nothing here has been checked against experiment or published data, and no grid-convergence study (GCI) has been run. Treat the force coefficients as the right order of magnitude for a bluff body, not as numbers to quote.
  • The mesh is coarse. Surface refinement and boundary-layer counts are below practice. Wall functions (y⁺ 30–100) mean the boundary layer is modelled, not resolved.
  • Steady RANS. Unsteady separation and vortex shedding in the wake are time-averaged away. A real wake is far less tidy than this.
  • The animation rides a frozen field. Particles follow a fixed, converged velocity field — these are streamlines, not pathlines. Nothing here evolves in time.
  • The 2D view is one plane only. Flow going around the sides of the body does not appear in it, which is why the 3D view exists alongside.
